## What it does

Press one key at the table. SpotShot captures the table, opens the lobby and captures it with
the payouts automatically, then combines the screenshots into one capture. Once your session is
over and poker clients are closed, it analyses every capture on your PC:

- **Generates hand setup .txt file** and the **tournament structure .json file** for each spot ready to import to HRC and GTO Wizard.
- **Every figure worked out:** player knockouts in BB, total chips in play, average mystery bounty are calculated automatically.
- **Anonymous mode** option to hide your and other players' names on the capture during the spot analysis process.
- **A spot library:** every spot described and sorted, found by position, stack, stage, hole cards,
  room or custom tag, with notes and a one-page PDF presentation editor for each spot. Send a spot, a search or the whole
  library to your friend or coach in one .zip.

Works with GGPoker, PokerStars, PartyPoker, 888poker, ACR, Winamax, WPT Global, CoinPoker,
iPoker (Red Star), PokerDom, Chico and QQPK.

## Install

1. Download **[SpotShot-installer.exe](https://github.com/spotshotgg/spotshot/releases/latest/download/SpotShot-installer.exe)**
   and run it. No installer wanted? Take the portable
   [SpotShot-installer.zip](https://github.com/spotshotgg/spotshot/releases/latest/download/SpotShot-installer.zip).
2. If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. SpotShot asks for administrator rights when it starts, so it can click the lobby button in
   poker clients that run as administrator.

Supported on Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Nothing else to install. Updates arrive in the app.

## Private by design

- While you play, SpotShot only takes screenshots. Nothing is analysed, and the library stays
  closed, until every poker client is closed.
- No account needed and no personal data is stored. Player names on the tables are never saved and do not appear in generated hand setup files.
- Your captures never leave your PC. SpotShot goes online only for updates and its periodic access check.
